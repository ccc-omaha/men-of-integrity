What Is Men of Integrity?

An honest room for an issue most men hide.

Men of Integrity (MOI) is part of the Renewal ministry at Christ Community Church in Omaha. We exist for men dealing with sexual brokenness, whether that's pornography, lust, or patterns you've tried to quit on your own and couldn't.

Shame thrives in secret. Healing happens in the light, alongside other men who get it. MOI gives you a safe place to be honest, connect with God in the middle of your struggle, and get practical tools you can use right away.

Open group. New men are welcome any Tuesday.

Our Mission

Five commitments guide every Tuesday.

This isn't a lecture or a quick fix. It's a community built on a few things we take seriously.

01

Safety

A place where men are loved and accepted as they are, not as they pretend to be.

02

Healing

Helping men meet God in the middle of their struggle, not after they've cleaned it up.

03

Honesty

Building an authentic life with nothing left to hide.

04

Education

Learning how recovery works and getting tools you can use immediately.

05

Accountability

Men who walk with you, keep you honest, and hold out real hope for purity.

New to Recovery?

Three first steps.

If you're reading this, something in you wants to change. That's the first step. Here are the next three.

1

Get honest with yourself

Take a free, private self-assessment. Answer truthfully. It's for you, and nobody else sees the results.

2

Show up on a Tuesday

Close the open doors
